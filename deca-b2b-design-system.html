01 What the audit found

Four blocking problems, all measured

The site is well built structurally — disciplined components, a real motion system with reduced-motion support, and a clear editorial voice. The problems are all in colour and type scale.

1 · Two design systems that share nothing

home.css and styles.css declare separate :root blocks with zero hex values in common. The marketing page runs a warm green (#05bd3b) on green-tinted neutrals (#102521 ink, #f8fbf8 paper); the auth pages run a punchy green (#04ca02) on blue-tinted neutrals (#092344 ink, #003571 sapphire). A visitor moving from the homepage to sign-up crosses a brand boundary. One of these two should win.

2 · The primary button label is unreadable in both systems

White on the marketing green #05bd3b measures 2.52:1. On the auth gradient it is worse — the light green stop #58ea60 against white measures 1.57:1, which is not a marginal miss but text you cannot read. Both need 4.5:1.

3 · Sixteen off-token greys, none of which pass

Across home.css the small labels are set in sixteen ad-hoc greys — #84948b, #a0aaa3, #97a6b3 and so on — at sizes between 8px and 11px. Every one fails 4.5:1; the worst lands at 2.30:1. Meanwhile the --muted token already defined in the same file passes at 4.54:1. The tokens are fine — the pages just don’t use them.

4 · Text colours used below their threshold

--green-dark at 4.17:1 is used for headline accent words and links; --blue at 4.46:1 for links; auth --green at 2.22:1. All three are brand colours being asked to do a job they cannot do at body size.

The one type decision to reverse

The system sets real interface copy at 8–11px — footer links, captions, form labels, the whole small-text layer. That is below any comfortable reading floor and is the root cause of the sixteen greys failing: at 8px, a colour needs more contrast, not less. Raise the floor to 12px for any text a visitor is meant to read, and reserve anything below that for genuinely non-text marks.

07 Rules

What to keep, what to change

Keep — one token set

Pick the marketing sheet’s green-tinted neutrals or the auth sheet’s sapphire-tinted ones, and delete the other. Two systems cannot both be the brand.

Keep — the motion system

Durations, easings and the reduced-motion block are disciplined and complete. Nothing here needs changing.

Keep — the arch

The 140px hero arch and 94px trust corner are the system’s one decisive flourish. Worth protecting.

Keep — one image, CSS for the rest

The whole homepage loads a single raster asset. Every mockup, chart and map is borders, gradients and inline SVG. That is the system’s strongest engineering decision — don’t let a redesign undo it by swapping in screenshots.

Change — the small-text floor

Raise real copy from 8–11px to 12px minimum. Sixteen failing greys are a symptom of the size, not just the hue.

Change — off-token colour

Every one of the sixteen greys should become --muted, which already passes. The tokens were never the problem.

Change — white on light green

Never place white text on #05bd3b, #04ca02 or #58ea60. Use the ink label, or deepen the fill.
